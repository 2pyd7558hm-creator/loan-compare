Actual rates, APRs, fees and payments depend on credit, property, loan amount, occupancy and market conditions, and are subject to underwriting approval. Rates can change daily.

APR is estimated and treats points, lender fees, mortgage insurance and financed upfront premiums (FHA upfront MIP 1.75%, VA funding fee 2.15% first use) as finance charges. Conventional PMI is shown until the balance reaches 78% of the purchase price. FHA annual MIP is shown for the life of the loan with less than 10% down, or 11 years otherwise. Cash to close shows down payment, points and lender fees, plus third-party fees, prepaids and escrow deposits when closing costs are itemized. Fees paid by the seller or lender are not included. Prepaids and escrow deposits are estimates. Taxes and insurance are estimates.
