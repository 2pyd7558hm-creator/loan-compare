For illustration only. This is not a loan offer, a commitment to lend, or a Loan Estimate. Figures are estimates based on the inputs shown. Actual rates, APRs, fees and payments depend on credit, property, loan amount, occupancy and market conditions, and are subject to underwriting approval. Rates can change daily.

APR is estimated and treats points, lender fees, mortgage insurance and financed upfront premiums (FHA upfront MIP 1.75%; VA funding fee for first use, 2.15% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% or more, 1.25% with 10% or more) as finance charges. Conventional PMI is an estimate and is shown until the balance reaches 78% of the purchase price. FHA annual MIP follows HUD's current table (Mortgagee Letter 2023-05), is figured on each year's average scheduled balance as HUD does, and is shown for 11 years with 10% or more down, otherwise for the life of the loan. Jumbo loans (over the FHFA 2026 conforming loan limit for the county) are shown with no mortgage insurance. Adjustable-rate (ARM) options are shown at their starting rate for the whole loan; the ARM vs fixed tab shows how the payment can change after the fixed period. Cash to close shows down payment, points and lender fees, plus third-party fees, prepaids and escrow deposits when closing costs are itemized. Fees paid by the seller or lender are not included. A seller credit is applied to closing costs, prepaids and any buydown, never to the down payment; seller credit limits shown are typical program limits and are estimates. A temporary buydown lowers the payment by 1 to 3 points of rate in the first years; its cost is the total of those payment reductions, and the APR is figured at the note rate. A second loan or down payment assistance reduces the cash needed for the down payment; a deferred second has no monthly payment and is usually repaid on sale or refinance. Prepaids and escrow deposits are estimates. Taxes and insurance are estimates.
