APR is estimated and treats points, lender fees, mortgage insurance and financed upfront premiums (FHA upfront MIP 1.75%; VA funding fee for first use, 2.15% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% or more, 1.25% with 10% or more) as finance charges. FHA annual MIP follows HUD's current table (Mortgagee Letter 2023-05), is figured on each year's average scheduled balance as HUD does, and is shown for 11 years with 10% or more down, otherwise for the life of the loan.
